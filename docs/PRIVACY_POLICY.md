## What PricePulse does
PricePulse lets you track prices of products you choose and alerts you when a price changes or reaches your target.

## Data we collect
Only when **you** click "Track Price" (or open the popup on a page you already track), PricePulse sends to our server:
- the product page URL, product name and price you are tracking, and the currency;
- your target price and tracking settings;
- a random identifier generated when you install the extension (not linked to your name, email or Google account);
- price observations over time (price, time, whether it came from your browser or our server).

The extension reads the page you are on **only** when you open the popup (it uses the activeTab permission). It does not read pages in the background and does not collect browsing history.

## What we do not collect
Names, email addresses, passwords, payment details, cookies, or the content of pages other than the product data above.

## How we use it
To store your tracked products and price history, to check prices on a schedule, and to send you notifications.

## Where it is stored and who has it
Data is stored on [hosting provider / region] and [MongoDB Atlas region]. We do not sell data or share it with third parties, except infrastructure providers that host the service for us. Our server also requests the product page URLs you track in order to read their prices.

## Retention and deletion
Removing tracking in the extension deletes that product and its price history from our server.
