# PricePulse Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 29, 2026  
**Operator:** PricePulse (independent project)  
**Contact:** bebugsfinder@gmail.com

## What PricePulse does

PricePulse is a browser extension that lets you track prices for products you choose. It records price history and can notify you when a tracked price changes or reaches your target.

## Data PricePulse handles

When you choose to track a product, PricePulse processes and sends to its backend:
- the product page URL, product name, price, and currency;
- your target price and tracking settings;
- a random identifier created for this extension installation, used to associate its tracked products with that installation; and
- price observations, including the price, time, and whether the observation came from your browser or a server check.

The extension reads product details from the active tab only when you open the extension popup. It does not collect your general browsing history or read pages in the background. The PricePulse server may request the URLs of products you chose to track to check their publicly available prices.

PricePulse does not ask for your name, email address, password, payment details, or cookies. If you contact support, the operator will receive the email address and information you include in your message.

## How data is used

PricePulse uses this information only to provide price tracking: store your selected products and price history, check tracked product pages on a schedule, synchronize extension data, and send requested price notifications. It is not sold or used for advertising, credit decisions, or lending.

## Storage and service providers

PricePulse is an independent project. Its backend is hosted by Render in Oregon (US West). The database is hosted by MongoDB Atlas on AWS in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1). Render and MongoDB Atlas process or store the information as infrastructure providers needed to run PricePulse. The backend also requests tracked product URLs to check prices.

## Retention and deletion

Tracked products and their price history remain on the server until you remove those products in the extension. Removing a product deletes its product record and price history. Uninstalling the extension alone does not delete data already stored on the server. PricePulse does not currently automatically delete inactive data.

To request deletion of all data associated with your installation, email **bebugsfinder@gmail.com** with your installation identifier, shown in PricePulse Settings. The operator will handle the request manually.

## Security

Requests between the extension and the backend use HTTPS. The project and its infrastructure providers use access controls, but no system can guarantee perfect security.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if PricePulse's data practices change. For questions or deletion requests, contact **bebugsfinder@gmail.com**.
